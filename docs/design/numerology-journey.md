# Discover Numerology journey

The existing Django route now contains six chapters: before written history, Mesopotamian records, the Pythagorean tradition, cultural symbolism, modern practice, and personal reflection with Åse. English and Norwegian content follow the existing language cookie. Existing resource and calculator links remain available.

The design uses midnight teal, antique gold, large editorial typography, arched image crops, chapter navigation, progressive disclosure and bounded scroll parallax. Content remains visible without JavaScript. Motion can be paused and respects reduced-motion preferences.

Historical references: The Metropolitan Museum of Art (collection item 325500) and Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Pythagoras. Images are original artistic interpretations, explicitly identified on the page; they are not archival evidence. Numerology is distinguished from documented mathematics and presented as symbolic interpretation.

## Image provenance

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. All six assets use landscape 3:2 composition, cinematic editorial illustration, deep midnight teal and bronze gold, realistic material textures, directional lighting; no UI, captions or watermarks. WebP derivatives are under `tall_project/static/journey/images/`.

- origins: prehistoric rocky coastline, starry night and crescent moon, grouped smooth stones and a tally stick.
- mesopotamia: clay accounting tablet and counting tokens, river and mudbrick city at twilight.
- pythagoras: ancient Greek seaside colonnade, monochord and pebbles arranged as a tetractys.
- traditions: imagined historical library, manuscripts and brass armillary sphere.
- modern: early twentieth-century writing desk, notebook, fountain pen and numerical chart.
- personal: contemporary Scandinavian desk, journal, brass numerals and Norwegian fjord.

Static assets are registered through STATICFILES_DIRS and collected by the existing deployment workflow. No new application dependencies, database migrations or calculator changes.
